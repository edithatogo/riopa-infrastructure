# Track: Rights, privacy and Māori data sovereignty framework

Track ID: `governance_maori_data_sovereignty_20260718`  
Phase: **Foundation**

## Goal

Embed lawful reuse, social licence, benefit, Māori rights and privacy decisions into source registration and publication gates.

## Dependencies

- `foundation_architecture_20260718`

## Scope

- Rights and redistribution status taxonomy.
- Māori data sovereignty trigger and review process.
- CARE/FAIR relationship and benefit/risk documentation.
- Aggregate-health privacy boundary and controlled-data escalation.
- Governance evidence in research objects.

## Out of scope

- Claiming consultation or Māori governance without participation.
- A generic checkbox as a substitute for governance.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Source registration cannot publish when redistribution status is unresolved.
- [ ] Trigger criteria and escalation roles are documented and reviewed with appropriate Māori expertise/partners.
- [ ] Research objects carry rights and governance decisions and limitations.
- [ ] Controlled-data work is separated architecturally from public-data releases.
- [ ] At least one spatial-health pilot completes documented governance review before publication.

## Risks

- Tokenistic governance.
- Overgeneralising public/open licences.
- Publishing harmful derived classifications.
