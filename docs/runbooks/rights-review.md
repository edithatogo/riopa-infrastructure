# Rights and legal-status runbook

Trigger this runbook when terms, licence, authority, legal status, or
redistribution conditions change or are uncertain.

1. Quarantine new incorporation and preserve the exact metadata and locator.
2. Reconcile the rights inventory, source approval, target representation, and
   expiry against the candidate artifact.
3. Record the decision, scope, permitted targets, exclusions, owner, and
   correction route.
4. Publish only when the required rights evidence is present and current.

Stop conditions: unclear authority, expired terms, restricted target, or a
material scope change. Do not infer permission from public availability.
