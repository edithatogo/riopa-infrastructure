# v0.4 source-rights disposition

This record distinguishes sources that can support the bounded v0.4 technical
preview from sources whose authority or publication rights are not evidenced.
A public URL does not by itself establish ownership, redistribution permission,
derivative-publication permission, authoritative status or completeness.

## Eligible bounded sources

- Wellington City 2024 District Plan zones: `CC-BY-3.0-NZ` is recorded for the
  bounded feature capture. The release must not infer statutory meaning or
  operative legal status.
- Ministry for the Environment National Planning Standards PDF: `CC-BY-4.0`
  is recorded for the official bytes. Automated legal interpretation remains
  out of scope.
- Ministry of Health certified public-hospital CSV: `CC-BY-4.0` is recorded.
  Certification does not establish service completeness or capacity.
- LINZ primary-parcels metadata: `CC-BY-4.0` is recorded for metadata use. A
  payload release still requires the exact layer's terms and version receipt.

The preferred v0.4 national-plus-council pair is the Ministry for the
Environment document or Ministry of Health CSV together with the Wellington
City District Plan zones. Exact source terms, attribution, version, capture and
digest receipts remain mandatory in the release object.

The current auditable pointers are
`config/source-registry/wp007-real-slice.yaml` and
`evidence/wp007-real-slice/manifest.json`; the release validator must verify the
manifest's recorded SHA-256 values before treating any listed artifact as
eligible.

## Excluded pending rights or authority evidence

- Wellington City Churton Park Village Supermarket layer: no licence is
  declared; payload acquisition and redistribution remain disabled.
- ArcGIS NZ ambulance-station prototype: a visible `CC-BY` label does not prove
  provider ownership, scope, provenance, national completeness or endorsement.
- Greater Wellington ambulance layer: licence, attribution, redistribution,
  update and withdrawal terms are unconfirmed.
- Health New Zealand, Hato Hone St John and Wellington Free Ambulance station,
  boundary or operational-location candidates: authoritative dataset identity,
  publication rights, privacy or sensitive-location constraints and correction
  routes are unconfirmed.

These exclusions do not block v0.4 when the release uses only the eligible
bounded pair and records the exclusions. A subagent panel may audit the rights
evidence and claims, but it cannot grant a missing licence or establish source
authority.
