# Optional Māori-specific governance and engagement guidance

This guidance describes a pathway for work that calls for Māori-specific
engagement. It is optional and is not a mandatory completion gate for this
track. It does not certify tikanga, consent or Māori approval.

## Trigger and triage

The source registry and decision framework flag data that is for, from or about
Māori, contains culturally sensitive geography or creates Māori-relevant
derived classifications. The programme owner then records the affected scope,
known relationships, proposed use, potential benefit and potential harm.

## Engagement sequence

1. Identify the relevant iwi, hapū, Māori organisation, data custodian or
   institutional governance contact without assuming that one representative
   speaks for all affected communities.
2. Share the proposed purpose, fields/geography, transformations, access path,
   retention, publication targets and withdrawal route in accessible form.
3. Invite advice on benefit, harm, tikanga, data sovereignty, interpretation,
   access conditions and acceptable granularity; record disagreement and
   uncertainty rather than averaging it away.
4. Record an attributed decision with role, evidence, scope, date, expiry,
   conflicts and conditions. A non-response is not approval.
5. Re-engage when purpose, population, geography, linkage, model or release
   target changes materially.

## Escalation and stop rule

Unresolved disagreement, missing relationship, unclear authority, foreseeable
group harm or an expired decision yields `review-required` and blocks the
affected capture, transformation, linkage or publication action. The source
and prior provenance remain preserved; no public release is made while blocked.

## Current state

The pathway is implemented as policy and schema guidance. No live engagement,
co-design or Māori governance approval is claimed for this development
checkout. The pathway may be activated when requested by affected parties, a
data custodian, law, licence, safety analysis or the declared project scope.
