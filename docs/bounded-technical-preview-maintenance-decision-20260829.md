# Bounded technical-preview maintenance decision

This successor decision records the recommended posture after publication of
the three rights-qualified Tier-A ArcGIS packets. RIOPA remains a bounded
regional, public-datasets-only, non-operational technical preview.

The decision does not promote beta, release candidate or stable-v1. Network,
timetable, facility-authoritative, national, clinical and dispatch claims
remain disabled. External participant evidence, elapsed beta/RC windows,
hosted recovery and scale measurements, and a tier-specific signed promotion
decision remain open.

The decision is reviewed on 2026-08-31 and earlier if scope, rights,
source-status, safety, integrity or operational conditions change. A failure
requires quarantine, correction or withdrawal and a successor decision before
restoration.
