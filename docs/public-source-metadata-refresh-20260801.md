# Public-source metadata refresh

**Checked:** 2026-08-01  
**Status:** metadata-only; no source payloads downloaded or redistributed.

The panel refreshed the public candidates in
`docs/public-ambulance-source-rights-matrix.md`. No candidate gained
authoritative national status or sufficiently clear redistribution rights.

| Candidate | Observation | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| [NZ Ambulance Station Locations prototype](https://www.arcgis.com/home/item.html?id=e5b22c07d197417495c8012e945c41da&sublayer=0) | Reachable; visible dataset update is 2024-06-14; remains explicitly a prototype and not provider authority. | Discovery/corroboration only; do not treat as national or operational evidence. |
| [Greater Wellington ambulance layer](https://mapping.gw.govt.nz/arcgis/rest/services/GW/Emergencies_P/MapServer/1) | Reachable and query-capable; regional point layer, but no visible licence, version timestamp or redistribution authority. | Request written rights and source authority before capture or publication. |
| [Wellington Free Ambulance region page](https://www.wfa.org.nz/about-us/our-region) | Public coverage context; no downloadable station dataset or reuse terms. | Context only; provider confirmation still required. |
| [Hato Hone St John locations](https://www.stjohn.org.nz/About-St-John/Our-Locations/) | Public locations map; no downloadable station dataset or licence observed. | Custodian route only. |
| [Health NZ ambulance performance reports](https://www.healthnz.govt.nz/publications/emergency-ambulance-service-national-performance-reports-2026) | Public aggregate performance reporting; no authoritative open station register. | Supporting context, not station authority. |
| [Stats NZ Meshblock 2026 service](https://services2.arcgis.com/vKb0s8tBIA3bdocZ/ArcGIS/rest/services/Meshblock_2026/FeatureServer) | Current geographic denominator service; exact release terms still require capture. | Supporting geography only. |
| [LINZ terms and licensing](https://data.linz.govt.nz/terms-of-use/) | Public terms; product-specific conditions and personal-data restrictions remain applicable. | Verify exact product terms before use. |

## Consequence

The ambulance source-authority gate remains open. The bounded regional,
non-authoritative and non-operational posture is unchanged. Any future source
acquisition must follow the fail-closed sequence in the rights matrix and retain
the custodian response, exact version, terms, payload hash and correction route.
