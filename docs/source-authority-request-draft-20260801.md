## Requested written answers

For each dataset, layer or map that you own or endorse, please answer the
following in writing and identify the responsible role:

1. Is this an authoritative source for ambulance stations, service areas or
   another explicitly defined concept? Who is the owner/custodian and what is
   the authoritative dataset or layer identifier?
2. What geography, population and time period are covered? List exclusions,
   known gaps, positional limitations and whether national completeness is
   endorsed.
3. What is the publication/version identifier, last update date and expected
   update cadence? How are corrections, late records, withdrawals and
   successor versions communicated?
4. May RIOPA capture the approved revision, preserve its metadata and payload,
   transform it into aggregate/non-identifying outputs, and publish those
   outputs? Please provide the exact licence, attribution wording, terms URL,
   derivative-use and redistribution conditions.
5. Which fields, coordinates, facilities or linkages are sensitive or
   restricted? Specify retention, access, de-identification, safety and
   disclosure requirements.
6. Who may approve acquisition and release, what evidence must be retained,
   and how should a correction, takedown or withdrawal request be authenticated
   and propagated?
7. What claims may RIOPA make publicly (for example, source-level authority,
   regional coverage or national coverage), and what claims are expressly
   prohibited?

## Requested response record

Please provide an authoritative URL or signed statement, named contact and
role, exact terms/licence reference, coverage and freshness description,
decision date and expiry/review date. If the organisation is not the custodian,
please identify the correct custodian and forwarding route.

## Fail-closed handling

Until a written response is received and recorded in the source-acquisition
approval template, RIOPA will retain only already-public metadata, will not
download or redistribute candidate payloads, and will make no national or
operational-safety claim. A non-response, ambiguous answer or stale decision is
recorded as `review-required`, not permission. The bounded regional,
non-authoritative technical-preview posture remains in force.
